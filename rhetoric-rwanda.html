So What
The question this page answers: Rwanda sells security — its army guards governments in the Central African Republic and Mozambique, where it protects the $20B Mozambique LNG restart. The US now sanctions that army over what UN experts and the US say is support for M23 in the DRC (Kigali denies it). Does the security-export model survive Western pressure — and who feels it if it does not?
