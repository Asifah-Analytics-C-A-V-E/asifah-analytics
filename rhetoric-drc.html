Three Fronts in the Eastkinetic ladder · L3+ needs two outlets
Each front is scored by the worst thing reported there this week, not by how much is written: L1 mention · L2 threat · L3 attack · L4 mass casualty · L5 a town, city or base taken, or Kinshasa attacked. A level of L3 or higher needs two or more outlets; one outlet alone is held at L2. All three fronts are standing (the M23 campaign has run since 2021, the eastern war far longer), so L3–L4 there is the condition, not news. Only a front can take the DRC to L5 — and a town genuinely taken this week does.

Deal vs Groundthe Washington and Doha tracks · against the fronts
Two peace tracks run at once: Washington (the DRC and Rwanda, US-brokered) and Doha (the government and the FRN/M23, Qatar-facilitated). This card counts the outlets reporting each track as holding, stalled, fraying or collapsing — and sets that against the fronts. "On paper only" means the deals are reported alive while two or more outlets report fighting at L3 or above.
