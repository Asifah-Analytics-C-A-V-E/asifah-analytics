How we got here, and 2026 so far
  • 2021 – 2024 Rwandan troops deploy to Cabo Delgado at Mozambique's request (2021) and to CAR under a bilateral deal alongside MINUSCA. Burundi closes its border and cuts ties (Jan 2024). The EU and Rwanda sign a raw-materials memorandum (Feb 2024). Paul Kagame is re-elected with 99% (Jul 2024). (various)
  • Jan – Feb 2025 M23 takes Goma and Bukavu. UN experts report Rwandan troops fighting alongside M23; Kigali denies backing the group and cites "defensive measures" against the FDLR. (Wikipedia: M23 campaign)
  • Jun – Dec 2025 A US-brokered DRC–Rwanda peace agreement (27 Jun) and the Washington Accords (4 Dec). (Al Jazeera)
  • Mar 2026 The US sanctions the Rwandan army and senior officials over support for M23; Kigali calls the sanctions "one-sided". Rwanda warns it may withdraw from Mozambique without funding as EU support wavers. (Al Jazeera · ISS)
  • May 2026 Ebola restrictions at the Rubavu–Goma border. Mozambique secures funding for the Rwandan mission. Rwandan ores, coffee and essential oils gain tariff-free access to China (1 May). (Africanews · Engineering News · KT Press)
  • Aug 2026 Inflation at 15.7%; the central bank raises its policy rate to 8.75%. Q2 merchandise exports up 51%, led by minerals. (IMF · KT Press)
  • Oct 2026 New Goma–Gisenyi restrictions after Ebola cases in southern Lubero (10 Oct). The US sanctions Rwanda's Ministry of Defence and Zigama Credit (28 Oct); Kigali rejects the allegations. (allAfrica · Africanews)
  • Nov 2026 The US announces Rwanda's exclusion from AGOA from January 2027 (2 Nov). Austria, Denmark, Germany, Greece and the Netherlands announce a planned "return hub" in Rwanda (16 Nov). (Critical Threats · Al Jazeera)

Contested claims, shown as such: UN experts and the US government say Rwanda supports M23 and that Congolese minerals pass through Rwandan exporters; Rwanda denies both and says the FDLR, which it calls a genocidal militia, threatens it from Congolese soil. This page reports both and adjudicates neither.

How to read this page

This is a sensor page for a hub. Rwanda is not in a crisis at home; what it does matters because of where it reaches. The humanitarian dial is refugees hosted; the financial tiles watch its contested exports and its import costs.

The analyst layer — the Rwanda wheel, with CAR, the DRC and Mozambique as spokes, and the ripple of each across the region — lives on the hub page.

Why Rwanda matters beyond Rwanda

The region. Rwanda's posture is the variable the eastern DRC war tracks most closely, and its rivalries with Burundi and Uganda play out on Congolese soil.

The continent. Rwanda sells security: its troops guard governments in CAR and Mozambique, where they made TotalEnergies' $20B gas project restartable. A sanctioned army is a test of whether that model survives Western censure.

The world. Coltan becomes tantalum for capacitors in phones, cars and AI hardware; Rwandan tungsten has been reported at up to a fifth of US primary tungsten concentrate use. Europe now wants Rwanda to take people it is removing. Few countries of 14 million sit on so many global supply lines at once.

Glossary
RDF · RPF
The Rwanda Defence Force · the Rwandan Patriotic Front, the ruling party, in power since 1994.
FDLR
Democratic Forces for the Liberation of Rwanda: a Hutu armed group in eastern Congo founded by people linked to the 1994 genocide. Kigali's stated reason for its "defensive measures".
M23 · AFC · FRN
The armed group that took Goma and Bukavu in 2025 · its political umbrella · Joseph Kabila's coalition (Oct 2026) that absorbed both.
3T
Tin, tantalum (from coltan) and tungsten — the "conflict minerals" of the Great Lakes, with gold the fourth.
AGOA
The US African Growth and Opportunity Act, which gives duty-free access to the US market; Rwanda is excluded from January 2027.
Northern · Central Corridor
The road routes to the sea through Uganda and Kenya (Mombasa) and through Tanzania (Dar es Salaam).
Kwibuka
"Remember": the annual commemoration of the genocide against the Tutsi, from 7 April.
MINEMA
Rwanda's Ministry in charge of Emergency Management, which runs the refugee camps with UNHCR.
