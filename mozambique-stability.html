Geography 2,500 km of coast · the war is in the far north
Maputo, the capital, is in the far south, about 2,000 km from Cabo Delgado. The gas fields lie offshore in the Rovuma Basin near the Tanzanian border; the onshore plants sit on the Afungi peninsula by Palma. The Nacala and Beira corridors carry trade for landlocked Malawi, Zambia and Zimbabwe. The pins mark the places this page talks about. Tap a pin for why it matters.

How we got here, and 2026 so far
  • 2017 – 2021 An Islamist insurgency begins with an attack on Mocímboa da Praia (5 Oct 2017) and later pledges to the Islamic State. Russian Wagner contractors deploy in 2019 and leave after losses. Insurgents storm Palma (March 2021); TotalEnergies declares force majeure. Rwandan troops arrive (July 2021) alongside a SADC mission. (Crisis Group · ACLED)
  • 2022 – 2024 Eni's offshore Coral Sul starts exporting LNG (late 2022). The SADC mission withdraws (2024). A disputed October 2024 election sets off the deadliest post-election crisis since independence; Balama graphite halts under force majeure in December. (Rio Times · Mining.com)
  • 2025 Daniel Chapo is inaugurated (15 Jan) and meets opposition leader Venâncio Mondlane (March); Mondlane founds Anamola (August). Eni takes the decision to build Coral Norte (October). TotalEnergies lifts force majeure (7 Nov); the UK and the Netherlands withdraw about $1.15B of export credit (December). (JPT · allAfrica · Mongabay)
  • Jan – Mar 2026 TotalEnergies announces a full restart (29 Jan); first LNG targeted for 2029. Three cyclones and floods; cholera passes 100 cases a day. The Mozal aluminium smelter goes into care and maintenance (16 Mar). Mozambique repays the IMF early, about $630M. (World Oil · Engineering News · Africanews)
  • Apr – Jun 2026 Rwanda says it has more than 6,300 troops in Cabo Delgado as EU funding ends in May; Mozambique secures the money to keep them. The battle for the Catupa base in Macomia begins (June). (allAfrica · Engineering News · Zitamar)
  • Jul – Oct 2026 Insurgents spread south and west, through Montepuez and into Niassa. Tanzania and Mozambique sign new defence protocols (October). Frelimo names Chapo its 2029 candidate (17 Oct). 7,029 people flee attacks in five weeks to 27 October. (Zitamar · Rio Times · IOM via Lusa)
  • Nov 2026 At least 22 killed in Muidumbe and Mueda (the Islamic State claimed 14, calling them Christians). Mondlane's trial is set to open at the Supreme Court (17 Nov). The cabinet approves ExxonMobil's revised Rovuma LNG plan (18 Nov). Eni begins drilling for Coral Norte. An IMF decision on a new programme is expected in December. (Zitamar · Energy Connects · ISS)

Names to keep straight: locally the insurgents are called "al-Shabaab" — no relation to Somalia's al-Shabaab. The Islamic State calls them its Mozambique province.

Why Mozambique matters beyond Mozambique

The region. Cabo Delgado borders Tanzania, and the insurgency has crossed before. The Beira and Nacala corridors are lifelines for landlocked Malawi, Zambia and Zimbabwe.

The continent. Islamic State's Mozambique province is one of its most active in Africa, with a growing maritime arm (34 sea-linked events by mid-November 2026). It is also the showcase of Rwanda's security-for-hire model.

The world. Four LNG projects would make Mozambique one of Africa's largest gas exporters around 2030, while Europe and Asia look for gas beyond Russia and the Gulf. Balama is the West's main graphite mine for battery anodes outside China. The Mozambique Channel is a shipping lane for the Cape route.

Glossary
ISM · "al-Shabaab"
Islamic State Mozambique, also called Ansar al-Sunna; locally "al-Shabaab" (unrelated to Somalia's).
FDS · Local Force
Mozambique's defence and security forces · the local militia folded into the army by a 2023 decree.
SAMIM
The SADC (Southern African) military mission in Cabo Delgado, 2021 – 2024.
LNG · FLNG · FID
Liquefied natural gas · a floating LNG plant · a final investment decision (the point of no return).
Frelimo · Renamo · Podemos · Anamola
The ruling party since 1975 · the former rebel opposition · the party that backed Mondlane in 2024 · Mondlane's own party, founded 2025.
N380
The main north–south road through Macomia to Palma; the insurgents' favourite ambush line.
MIMO
The Bank of Mozambique's policy rate (9.25% in September 2026).
