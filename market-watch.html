What This Is

A global markets board plus a Black Swan fragility detector measuring endogenous bubble-risk convergence against historical episodes (1987–2026). Market fragility is the dry forest; the GPI kinetic/diplomatic axes are the lightning. Compound reads surface at GPI altitude.

Methodology

Yahoo Finance (monthly closes), FRED (yield curve, credit spreads), Shiller CAPE. Pattern memory + Jaccard similarity. Open-source only. Convergence framing, never prediction.
