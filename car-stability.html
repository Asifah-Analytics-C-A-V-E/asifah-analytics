What the Central African Republic Depends On Dated facts · reviewed 22 Nov 2026
CAR is landlocked, imports almost everything up one road from Douala, and is guarded by foreigners: Russian fighters, Rwandan troops and UN peacekeepers. Its gold and diamonds pay for that protection more than they pay the state. Read the four panels together: the road out, the guards, who holds the minerals, and the fronts. Each is a dependency someone else can pull.

2026 so far — what changed this year
  • Dec 2025 – Jan 2026 Touadéra won a third term with 76% after a 2023 referendum removed term limits; the main opposition boycotted. On election day (28 Dec) the Azande Ani Kpi Gbe (AAKG) militia seized Bambouti on the South Sudan border, and on 4 Jan it attacked and disabled Zémio's hospital, which UN peacekeepers now guard. (Al Jazeera · ADF)
  • Feb 2026 US Africom completed a second joint assessment with the CAR army and did not rule out a permanent office; the EU's training mission (EUTM) continues. Russia had asked Bangui for about $15M a month for Africa Corps. (DW via allAfrica)
  • Mar 2026 Touadéra sworn in for his third term (30 Mar). Reported formal absorption of Wagner's CAR operation into Africa Corps around the inauguration. A mine collapse killed 8. (The Africa Report)
  • Jun 2026 GI-TOC: nearly all of CAR's declared gold exports since 2023 went to the UAE; Africa Corps is estimated to benefit from about $180M a year of illicit gold. (Semafor)
  • Jul 2026 About ten days of fuel queues in Bangui; street fuel at 1,500 FCFA a litre against 1,100 official. A UN report described Russian fighters exploiting CAR's conflict economy. (Radio Ndeke Luka · ADF)
  • Aug 2026 Rwandan instructors graduated 670 CAR soldiers at Camp Kassaï in Bangui, with Touadéra presiding. (allAfrica / New Times)
  • Oct 2026 An official fuel price rise; then more than 100 trucks stranded at Garoua-Boulaï as Cameroonian drivers struck over harassment on the CAR side (13–15 Oct). The Wall Street Journal reported about 500 Wagner men still guarding Touadéra and run by Prigozhin's son, with CAR resisting Moscow's push to replace them with Africa Corps (19 Oct). (Rio Times · WSJ via Kyiv Post)
  • Nov 2026 The UN Security Council unanimously renewed MINUSCA to 15 Nov 2027, adjusting troop levels, amid the UN's cash crisis (13 Nov). (Africanews)

Conflicting reports, shown as such: one account says Wagner was absorbed into Africa Corps in March 2026; a later one (October) says Wagner still guards the president. Which is true is the rhetoric tracker's first question, not this page's.

How to read this page

This is a sensor page. Every figure is counted by somebody else and carries that body's name and date. Where a source did not report, the page says not reporting, never zero.

The humanitarian dial is DISPLACED ABROAD: more Central Africans are refugees in other countries (~668,000) than are displaced at home (~442,000) — about one person in five in all. The second dial is the Sudanese refugees at Korsi, because it is the one flow set by a neighbour's war.

"What CAR depends on" is the page's one interpretive frame, and it is deliberately made of dated facts rather than scores. What those dependencies mean for stability is the rhetoric page's job.

Why CAR matters beyond CAR

The region. CAR sits between three wars — Sudan to the east, the DRC to the south, and the Lake Chad and Sahel insurgencies to the north-west — and its north-east is a corridor for Sudan's war economy (gold, fuel, arms).

The continent. CAR is where Russia's African model began in 2018: a security guarantee for the president, paid in mining concessions, with political advisers attached. It is now also where that model is being nationalised (Africa Corps) and contested (Wagner's heirs). Rwanda has built a second, smaller version of the same offer here and in Mozambique.

The world. Gold that leaves through Dubai, diamonds under a partial Kimberley Process embargo, a UN peacekeeping mission renewed while the UN runs short of cash, and the United States quietly returning (Africom) to a country Russia treats as its showcase.

The CFA franc peg — read the peg, not the tick

CAR uses the Central African CFA franc (XAF), issued by the BEAC for the six CEMAC states — a different currency from the West African franc used in Niger, Mali and Burkina Faso, though both are fixed to the euro at 655.957. XAF/USD therefore moves only as EUR/USD moves.

The signal is the peg itself and the BEAC's reserves, which depend on the region's oil exporters (Cameroon, Congo, Gabon, Equatorial Guinea, Chad), not on CAR. (BEAC / CEMAC)

Glossary
Wagner · Africa Corps
Wagner: the private Russian military company in CAR since 2018, founded by Yevgeny Prigozhin (killed 2023). Africa Corps: the Russian Ministry of Defence force that has taken over most of Wagner's African network. In CAR, reports conflict on how far the handover has gone.
FACA
Forces armées centrafricaines, the national army, trained by Russian, Rwandan and EU instructors.
MINUSCA
The UN peacekeeping mission in CAR since 2014; mandate renewed to 15 November 2027.
CPC
Coalition des patriotes pour le changement, the rebel coalition formed in December 2020; weakened but present in the north-west and north-east.
3R · UPC
Retour, Réclamation et Réhabilitation (north-west) and Union pour la paix en Centrafrique (centre and east): armed groups that signed a ceasefire in N'Djamena in 2025 and announced they were disbanding.
AAKG
Azande Ani Kpi Gbe, a Zande militia in Haut-Mbomou (south-east), first armed by Wagner in 2024, now fighting the army and its Russian partners.
Ndassima · Midas Ressources
CAR's largest gold mine, near Bambari, held by a Wagner-linked company.
Diamville
Wagner-linked diamond buying company, sanctioned by the US, reported to route stones to the UAE.
Kimberley Process
The international diamond certification scheme; CAR exports only from certified "green zones" since the 2013 crisis.
Transhumance
The seasonal movement of herds from Chad and Sudan into CAR, December–April; a recurring trigger of violence.
CEMAC · BEAC
The Central African economic and monetary community, and its central bank, which issues the XAF.
IPC · DTM
The 1–5 acute food-insecurity scale · IOM's displacement counts.
