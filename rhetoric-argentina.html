About

Asifah Analytics tracks convergence, not prediction: which open-source signals are present, how strong the evidence is, and what they mean.

Data Sources

La Nación, Infobae, Buenos Aires Times, MercoPress (RSS); Google News in Spanish and English; GDELT; NewsAPI; the shared Hizballah Latin America module.

Methodology

7 actors in 6 vectors. Volume alone tops out at "elevated"; an event counts only when 2+ separate outlets report it as fact; one outlet = approaching; rumour and hedged wording never lift the level. L5 is reserved for kinetic ladders.

Convergence indicators, not predictions. Asifah reports which open-source signals are present — it does not forecast outcomes. Not for operational use.
