📚 KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

⚓ NATO Swing State

Ankara pursues "strategic autonomy": NATO membership, S-400s, Russia trade, and Gulf finance held simultaneously. The analytical signal is divergence -- autonomy rhetoric rising while Alliance cooperation falls -- not membership in either camp.

🗺️ Ottoman-Space Influence Playbook

Documented pattern: perimeter rhetoric, religious-cultural penetration (Diyanet, TIKA), economic stakes, defense cooperation agreement, then presence by invitation. Precedents: Northern Cyprus, Syria operations, Libya 2020, Somalia, Balkans soft power.

🇸🇾 Syria Footprint (Lead Indicator)

Turkey already maintains troops, proxy networks, and economic influence in northern Syria -- far deeper than any Lebanon presence. Sharp rises in Syria rhetoric have historically preceded broader regional ambition; this is the lead indicator vector.

🇱🇧 Lebanon Vector

Influence instruments observed to date are soft-power class: aid, education, religious institutions in Tripoli and Akkar, Turkmen community outreach. Watch items: reconstruction and port concessions, defense cooperation language, "buffer zone" framing.

⚖️ Constitutional Clock

Current term math requires a new constitution or early elections for Erdogan to run again (~2028). Analysts assess election proximity is suppressing appetite for war entanglement -- a timing condition, treated as a multiplier rather than a standalone signal.

🕌 Kurdish File & Domestic Pressure

PKK dissolution process, SDF integration in Syria, and fears of a Kurdish corridor in any Iran-collapse scenario. Domestically: opposition crackdown (Imamoglu case) and lira/inflation stress -- protest-risk and economic sensors feed here.
